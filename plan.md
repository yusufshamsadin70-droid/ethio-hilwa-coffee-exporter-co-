## Plan for Ethiopian Coffee Export Company Website

**Objective:** Create a modern, elegant, and professional website for an Ethiopian coffee exporting company.

**Key Features:**
- **Color Palette:** Warm colors inspired by coffee (brown, gold, cream).
- **Animations:** Smooth scrolling animations, smooth transitions, and hover effects.
- **Overall Style:** Minimal, luxury, clean, professional.

**Sections:**
1.  **Hero Section:**
    *   Background: Image of an Ethiopian coffee farm.
    *   Content: Compelling headline, call to action.
2.  **About Section:**
    *   Content: Explanation of Ethiopian coffee heritage.
3.  **Product Section:**
    *   Content: Showcase specific coffee beans like Sidamo, Yirgacheffe, and Harrar.
    *   Visuals: High-quality images of coffee beans/packaging.
4.  **Export Services:**
    *   Content: Detail export services and global shipping capabilities.
5.  **Certifications & Quality Assurance:**
    *   Content: Highlight quality standards and certifications.
6.  **Contact Section:**
    *   Content: Include contact options for WhatsApp and email.

**Implementation Notes:**
- The website should have a premium business look and feel.
- Ensure responsiveness across different devices.
- The `frontend_engineer` will be responsible for the implementation.
- Prior to writing code, the `frontend_engineer` must run `generate_images_bulk` to create necessary assets.
